Your plans, your account.

Daytrail prototype privacy notice · Updated 26 October 2026

Planning data

Device-only trips are saved in this browser. Account trips—including names, dates, activities, notes, custom places and Google Place IDs—are stored in Supabase Postgres and associated with your authenticated user ID. New trips in the account library sync automatically when connected. Existing device trips are uploaded only when you choose to move them. Local drafts and pending saves are stored in IndexedDB so temporary connection failures do not lose edits. Clearing browser storage removes those local copies and any changes that have not synced.

Signing in does not automatically upload device trips. Account libraries and pending drafts are separated by user ID. Signing out hides account trips and removes synced account caches. If you choose to keep pending drafts on this device, they remain available after signing back into the same account. Device-only trips and the original pre-migration recovery copy remain in this browser. Browser storage is not encrypted by Daytrail and is not a separate security boundary from access to your browser profile.

Place names, addresses, map coordinates, photos, ratings, opening hours and journey responses retrieved from Google are used in memory during your visit. Saved Google places are looked up again when needed. User-entered custom place details remain part of your saved plans.

Optional Google sign-in

Where configured, Daytrail uses Google and Supabase Authentication to sign you in. Google shares basic identity information such as your Google user ID, email address, name and profile image when available. Supabase stores the authentication account and manages sessions. Daytrail uses this information to display your account, keep you signed in and restrict access to your account trips; your Google password is not shared with Daytrail.

The sign-in session is stored in this browser. Authentication requests also send network information to Google and Supabase. Signing out does not delete your authentication account or local trip plans. This version has no in-app account deletion control.

Google's handling of personal information is described in its Privacy Policy. Supabase provides information about its services in its Privacy Policy. Supabase processes Daytrail authentication and cloud trip data on behalf of the app operator.

Google Maps

Maps, search and journeys use Google Maps Platform. Loading a map connects your browser to Google. Searches send the search text and destination or selected map area; routing sends selected locations, transport mode and, for transit, departure date and time. Google also receives the network information associated with these requests. Daytrail does not send your trip notes to Google for routing.

Google processes this information under the Google Privacy Policy. Use of Google Maps is subject to the Google Maps/Google Earth Additional Terms of Service.

Sharing and backups

A snapshot link contains a copy of the planning data in its URL fragment. Anyone you share that link with who has access to the hosted prototype can read or copy the included plans. A downloaded HTML itinerary is a standalone file for reading and printing.

A JSON backup contains editable local trip data and Google place IDs. It excludes your sign-in session and fetched Google place details. Importing a backup adds its trips to the selected library. In the account library, those imported trips will sync to Supabase; in the device library, they remain local. Keep downloaded files private if they contain personal plans or notes.

Deletion and recovery

Deleted trips are retained in Trash and can be restored. Deletion is synchronized so an older device cannot silently recreate a deleted trip. This version does not permanently purge trashed trips automatically. It has no in-app account deletion control. Existing downloaded backups and shared snapshot copies are not affected by deleting a trip.

Hosting

If you access Daytrail through ChatGPT, OpenAI provides hosting and access services covered by its Privacy Policy. If you access a Vercel-hosted copy, Vercel provides website hosting; see its Privacy Notice. Hosting providers receive network information needed to serve the website. Signing into Daytrail does not grant access to a separately restricted hosted site.
