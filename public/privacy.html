Your plans, your browser.

Daytrail prototype privacy notice · Updated 26 October 2026

Planning data

Your trip names, dates, activities, notes and Google place IDs are saved in local storage in this browser. This prototype does not synchronize trips to a Daytrail account. Clearing browser storage removes these local copies.

Place names, addresses, map coordinates and journey responses retrieved from Google are used in memory during your visit. Saved Google places are looked up again when needed.

Google Maps

Maps, search and journeys use Google Maps Platform. Loading a map connects your browser to Google. Searches send the search text and destination; routing sends selected locations, transport mode and, for transit, departure date and time. Google also receives the network information associated with these requests. Daytrail does not send your trip notes to Google for routing.

Google processes this information under the Google Privacy Policy. Use of Google Maps is subject to the Google Maps/Google Earth Additional Terms of Service.

Sharing and hosting

A snapshot link contains a copy of the planning data in its URL fragment. Anyone you share that link with who has access to the hosted prototype can read or copy the included plans. A downloaded itinerary is a standalone file you control.

The prototype is hosted through OpenAI Sites and requires access through ChatGPT. OpenAI's hosting and account services are covered by its Privacy Policy.
