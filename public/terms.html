Planning with Daytrail

Prototype terms of use · Updated 26 October 2026

About this prototype

Daytrail helps you organize personal travel plans. It does not make bookings or purchase tickets. Notes about reservations are entered by you; sample bookings are examples.

Travel times are estimates. Availability, transport schedules, opening hours and journey conditions can change. Missing journeys are shown as unavailable and are excluded from the known travel total. Check arrangements with the relevant providers before travelling.

Maps and place information

Google Maps content is provided through Google Maps Platform. By using these features you are also bound by the Google Maps/Google Earth Additional Terms of Service and Google's Terms of Service. Respect Google and third-party attributions displayed with this content.

Your plans and account

You choose the information you enter and share. Device-only trips stay in your browser. Account trips sync to Supabase when connected, with access restricted to your signed-in account. Existing device trips are moved only when you choose. Unsynced changes remain dependent on this browser until the server confirms saving. Deleted trips are retained in recoverable Trash. Signing out hides account trips; explicitly retained drafts remain tied to the original account. Keep an editable JSON backup before clearing browser storage or moving to another website address or device. A snapshot contains the planning data at the time it was shared; later changes do not update it. A standalone HTML itinerary is for reading and printing; a JSON backup can be imported as editable plans. Google place details and route estimates are retrieved when you use the app and are not included in these planning exports.

See Daytrail's privacy notice for how browser storage, sign-in, backups and Google Maps requests work.
